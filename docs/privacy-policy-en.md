**Selfer.** (the "Company") operates the mobile application **AngryMoti** (the "App"). We take your privacy seriously and comply with applicable privacy laws. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and how it is stored and deleted.

## 1. Information We Collect

| Item | When Collected | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Email address | Sign-up / sign-in (Email, Google, Apple) | Account identification and authentication |
| Name (optional) | When provided by social sign-in (Google, Apple) | Profile display |
| Points and penalties (score ledger) | When a session is completed or stopped | Activity records and statistics |
| Activity schedules and session records | While using the App | Providing App features |

In **Guest Mode**, no account information is collected, and all data is stored only on your device.

## 2. Camera Recordings (Timelapse)

- Timelapse videos recorded with the front camera during a session are stored **only on your device** and are **never uploaded to our servers**.
- Unless you explicitly save a recording on the session results screen, it is **deleted automatically and immediately**.
- While recording, files are stored with iOS Data Protection (full encryption).

> 💡 **Camera and On-Device Presence Detection:** AngryMoti uses face and body (pose) detection solely to determine whether you are in front of the camera (i.e., away detection) during a focus session. This processing happens entirely **on your device, in real time**; camera frames are processed in memory and **immediately discarded**. We do not store, collect, or transmit any facial data (face images, landmarks, templates, etc.), we do not **identify individuals**, and we detect only whether a person is present. This data is never shared with **any third party**, and is never sent to or stored on a server.

## 3. Use of Information and Third Parties

- We use the information we collect only for the purposes described above.
- We use **Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore)** for sign-in and data storage. That processing is subject to Google's privacy policy.
- We do not sell your personal information or share it with third parties for advertising purposes.

## 4. Retention and Deletion

- When you **delete your account**, your account information (email, name), on-device data (schedules, sessions, recordings), and **all data stored on our servers (including the score ledger) are immediately and permanently erased**. Deleted data cannot be recovered and is not retained in any form.
- You can delete your account at any time in the App under **My Page → Profile & Subscription → Delete Account**.

## 5. Subscriptions (In-App Purchases)

- AngryMoti Membership subscriptions are billed through the Apple App Store. The App does not collect or store any payment information (such as card numbers).

## 6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or ask us to stop processing it, at any time. You can exercise these rights immediately using the in-app account deletion feature.

## 7. Deletion Procedures

- Personal information is deleted without delay once the purpose of collection has been fulfilled or when you delete your account.
- Electronic personal information is **immediately deleted using irreversible methods**. (The Company does not keep personal information in paper or other non-electronic form.)
- The Company does not keep separate archives or retain data beyond its stated purposes.
